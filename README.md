# ESANN 2027: preprocessing effects on lesion reliance in diabetic retinopathy classification

Data, figures and scripts behind the figures and numbers of the ESANN 2027 submission. The paper text is not part of this repository.

**Lesion reliance A** = mean over the 524 test images with annotated lesions of `p(control blurred) − p(lesions blurred)`, in probability points (1 point = 0.01), where `p` is the predicted probability of referable DR, the lesions are blurred with a Gaussian (σ = 10 px at 299 px) and the control is the same lesion shapes displaced to a lesion-free area.

## Layout

| Folder | Content |
|---|---|
| `docs/PARA_O_ARTIGO.md` | Facts, numbers, caveats and limitations per figure and result (written in Portuguese). Start here. |
| `data/` | CSV/JSON/TXT behind every figure and number: per-pipeline results (`pipelines_long.csv`, `pipelines_summary.csv`), filter effects, correlations, lesion types, control overlap and sensitivity, decomposition of A (`A_decomposicao*`), DDR results and phase 3 summary. |
| `figures/paper/` | Final PDFs of Figures 1–4 under the file names used in the LaTeX (width 12.17 cm, Latin Modern font), plus `png/` previews and a grayscale preview. |
| `figures/table_chains/` | LaTeX table (`tabela_chains.tex`) and the five thumbnails that replace the former Figure 5. The network table is `data/tabela_redes.tex`. |
| `verification/` | Value found vs. expected and method for the reported numbers. |
| `scripts/` | Python and R scripts that produce the data and figures. `make_figuras_v8.R` (Figure 1), `make_figuras_v9.R` (Figure 2) and `make_figuras_v10.R` (Figures 3 and 4) produce the final figures (`v3`–`v5` are helpers). |
| `fonts/` | "LM Fig": Latin Modern Roman 10 plus ρ, ≥, ≤, ■, ●, ▲ from Latin Modern Math; needed only to regenerate the figures. |

## Not included

- Fundus image panels (FGADR and DDR images used in Figures 1, 2 and 6). They are regenerated by `scripts/make_paineis_v3.py` and `scripts/make_extremas.py` from the public datasets.
- Trained models and the raw per-run ablation outputs from the cluster (the per-image ablation table is in `data/ablation_per_image.csv`).
- Scripts expect the original project layout (`hcpa/esann_materiais`, `hcpa/esann_figs`) and the cluster results folder; they are provided for transparency, not as a one-command pipeline.

## Datasets

FGADR (1,842 fundus images with lesion masks) and DDR, both public datasets; see their original licenses.
